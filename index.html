01.

about me

I'm Gedela Hemanth — an Electrical & Electronics Engineering student at JNTU-GV (R23, 3rd Year) and a self-taught cybersecurity researcher. My whole attack lab runs on an Android phone via Termux — 215+ apt packages, custom-built tools, no proot, no VM.

My security interests span the full spectrum: red teaming (offensive — pentesting, WiFi auditing, recon) and blue teaming (defensive — hardening, analysis, monitoring). I'm building toward CTF practice, bug bounty platforms (HackerOne / Bugcrowd), and government cybersecurity roles like CERT-In Scientist-B and DRDO.

On the build side, I create open-source tools that turn Android into a real dev + security workstation — from AI coding on Termux (Claude-termux) to WiFi audit toolkits (NetForge / DWK) to AI assistants (J.A.R.V.I.S. PWA). I also work on defence tech research: X-band radar signal absorbers for MSME submissions, currently at ₹350 prototype stage.

When I'm not breaking or building, I'm creating content — a cybersecurity series in Telugu + English (Tenglish) for YouTube/Shorts, turning complex attacks into 60-second lessons anyone can follow, and documenting everything on my blog.
